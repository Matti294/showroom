Ontos hero — five directions, one decision

Hi Isabel — before we plan the Copenhagen shoot, the one question only you can answer: what should the very first image of Ontos be? We ran the question through a five-perspective expert review — minimal-web curation (with minimal.gallery as the bar), Nordic art direction, brand strategy, two user voices (a sceptical seeker and a seasoned practitioner), and motion design. Their verdicts are quoted under each variant, including on our own proposals.

Everything here is input, not a decision. You are the design authority — C, D and E are proposals, and the final image for any of them would be a real photograph from the shoot.

The emotional arc — three additions, independent of the hero choice

Matthias asked a good question: is there room for more emotion — change, lightness, the longing to get away? We think yes, and none of it needs a new hero. It maps onto the page you already designed:

Change

The strongest image of change is light that changes — variant C performs it, and your photo brief already contains its most Danish form: light after rain. Wet asphalt, a sky tearing open. Change as weather, not as metaphor — worth shooting deliberately at the shoot.

Lightness

The brightest asset in the pool is the linen against the sky — sun through fabric, joy without a single smile. Lightness in Danish: light instead of grinning. It belongs in the bright sections and the content tiles (Journal, Academy), together with your gulls-over-water motif — not in the hero, which stays the dark pole of your rhythm.

The longing to get away — fulfilled

Ontos sells arrival, not escape. But your harbour swimmer already resolves that tension: a woman swims in the harbour basin at eight in the morning, before the city works. That is escape into one's own city — “Copenhagen, seen differently”, literally. We propose treating her as the emotional climax of the page and of the film: the page then ends on the strongest feeling, not merely the calmest.

The cheapest lever: the eight words

Your rule holds: the image carries arrival, the language carries departure. Today the eight hero words are all states (Rest, Presence, Clarity…). Swapping a few for words of longing and beginning — Curiosity, Courage, Beginnings, Wonder — gives the hero pull without changing a pixel. Try the “Words” toggle above to compare both sets live on every variant.

What to look for: do the eight words stay readable where the image turns bright? Does the claim sit on calm ground? Does the image survive the 9:16 crop? And does it hold the dark pole of your light–dark rhythm?

Open questions the panel raised for you:

  1. Rotating words or a standing sentence? The curator's view: featured minimal sites are typographically still — he would let the eight words change once, or not at all.
  2. Which word set? States only, or with the longing words — see the toggle.
  3. A quiet category subline? Strategy and both user voices asked for one line that says what Ontos is („A curated network of practitioners · Copenhagen") — try the toggle above.
  4. If C, D or E wins: does “is in the air.” stay? It was written over air. Over light or water it may still hold — your call.
